# Hotel Booking Demand Analysis & Business Insights Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project performs an end-to-end Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on hotel booking demand data for City Hotels and Resort Hotels. The objective is to identify guest behavior, booking trends, pricing patterns, seasonality, market segments, and cancellation drivers that can help hotel management make data-driven business decisions.

---

## Dataset

Dataset: Hotel Booking Demand Dataset

The dataset contains hotel booking information including:

* Hotel type
* Guest country
* Arrival month
* ADR (Average Daily Rate)
* Room type
* Meal preferences
* Market segments
* Special requests
* Booking cancellations

---

## Key Insights

* Portugal generated the highest number of hotel guests.
* City Hotels achieved higher average ADR compared to Resort Hotels.
* Resort Hotel pricing peaked significantly during July and August.
* Online Travel Agencies contributed the largest share of bookings.
* Guests with more special requests showed lower cancellation tendencies.
* August recorded the highest cancellation volume.
* Resort Hotel guests generally stayed longer than City Hotel guests.
